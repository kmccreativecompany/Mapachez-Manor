# Ghosts, guest gifts and the dress code (GHOSTS v1)

## Friendly ghosts
Some guests at Mapachez Manor are ghosts. Nobody at the manor thinks this is unusual, and that is the joke.

- **The Tanakas, Room 205.** Mr. Hiroshi Tanaka is travelling with his wife, Emiko. Emiko is a ghost. They are both raccoons (one species, as a married couple should look): Hiroshi is lavender, and Emiko is a pale, see-through raccoon.
  They drift along the guest corridor. Tap them for a little exchange between the two of them.
  Once a day there is a quick service moment. The lesson is real: every guest in a party gets the
  same care (their own key, their own warm cup, their side of the bed, a proper table for two).
  Best answer: +3 tips into the usual tip jar.
- **Two lobby chats:** the Tanakas and his 212 holiday photos, and Ngozi Eze and Lars Lindqvist (both ghosts)
  comparing the politest way upstairs (knock on the wall first).
- **Look:** the same pixel chibi kit, pale and soft, a little see-through, floating with feet fading
  into a wisp, with a soft moonlit glow. Set with `ghost:1` in `CAST_PINS`.
- **Tone rules:** sweet and light, never sad or scary. No words about death, widows, graves or haunting
  (a test checks). Traits are shown through behaviour.
- Pins: Party of Two (help the Tanakas 4 times), Nobody Minds (secret, say hello 7 times).
- Save key `mapachez_ghosts_v1`.

## Gifts come from thankful guests
For ethics, gifts never come from the manager. Every gift box shows who it is from (with their face)
and ends with a short signed thank-you note.

- Good or perfect quick shift: a guest you gave a best answer to in that shift.
- Full tip jar: one guest "and a few other guests".
- Mystery guest guessed right: that mystery guest.
- Finishing the HMS lessons: a guest you helped at the desk.
- Sometimes a ghost guest ("I wrapped it myself. It took a while.").
- Delores's cottage visit now brings a guest's thank-you card instead of a pie.

## Dress code
At work (the manor and the festival) the player wears their uniform. Gift outfits are worn off the
clock only, at home in the cottage. The closet explains this, buttons say "Wear at home", and when
something is set aside there is a "See it at home" button.
